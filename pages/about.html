Our Horror Story

Welcome, brave soul, to Lord Grimley's Manor – your premier destination for premium Halloween masks, horror props, and exclusive collectibles. What began as a passion for the macabre has grown into one of the internet's most trusted sources for high-quality horror merchandise.

We are proud authorized retailers of Trick or Treat Studios, bringing you officially licensed masks from your favorite horror films – from classics like Texas Chainsaw Massacre and Halloween to modern terrors and everything in between.

What We Offer

  • Premium Latex Masks – Professional-grade masks with incredible detail and durability
  • Lord Grimley Exclusives – Limited edition pieces found nowhere else
  • Horror Collectibles – Figures, props, and memorabilia for serious collectors
  • Halloween Decorations – Transform your home into a haunted manor
  • Special Effects Makeup – Professional FX supplies for the ultimate transformation
  • Costume Accessories – Gloves, hands, and finishing touches

Our Promise

Every item that leaves the Manor is inspected for quality. We understand that our customers are fellow horror enthusiasts who demand authenticity and craftsmanship. That's why we only carry products we would proudly display in our own collection.
