Buy direct from TOTS if you want one specific item. Choose Lord Grimley's for curated selection, exclusive masks, personalized service, and access to rare and discontinued items.

Understanding the Relationship

Here's something many horror fans don't realize: Lord Grimley's Manor is an authorized retailer of Trick or Treat Studios products. We love TOTS—they make some of the best licensed horror masks in the industry. But we're not competitors; we're partners in bringing premium horror to collectors.

Think of it like this: Trick or Treat Studios is the brewery making craft beer. Lord Grimley's Manor is the specialty bottle shop that curates the best brews, stocks rare vintages, and employs staff who can tell you exactly which beer pairs with your horror movie marathon.

Product Selection

Trick or Treat Studios

TOTS focuses on what they do best: creating screen-accurate, officially licensed horror masks and props. Their catalog includes masks from Halloween, Texas Chainsaw Massacre, Nightmare on Elm Street, Child's Play, Evil Dead, and many more. Quality is consistently high, with detailed sculpting and professional paint work.

  • Strength: Direct access to full TOTS catalog when in stock
  • Strength: First access to new releases
  • Limitation: Only TOTS-manufactured products
  • Limitation: Popular items sell out quickly

Lord Grimley's Manor

We curate the best of TOTS alongside products from Ghoulish Productions, our own Lord Grimley Exclusive line, and rare/discontinued masks that serious collectors seek. Our selection is smaller but intentionally curated—every item in our crypt has been hand-selected for quality and collectibility.

  • Strength: Multi-brand curation (TOTS, Ghoulish, exclusives)
  • Strength: Rare and discontinued masks available
  • Strength: Lord Grimley Exclusive designs found nowhere else
  • Limitation: Smaller selection than manufacturer direct

Bottom line: Choose TOTS direct if you need a specific current-production item. Choose Lord Grimley's for curated quality, exclusives, and rare finds.

Pricing

Trick or Treat Studios sets MSRP for their products, and authorized retailers like Lord Grimley's generally honor those prices. Where we differ:

  • Clearance: Our Clearance Crypt offers up to 65% off select items—deals you won't find manufacturer-direct
  • Bundles: We create exclusive bundles combining masks with accessories for better value
  • Rare Items: Discontinued masks may carry premium pricing based on collectibility

For current-production items at standard pricing, both options are comparable. For deals on clearance or access to rare items, Lord Grimley's provides options TOTS can't.

Customer Service

Trick or Treat Studios

TOTS provides standard manufacturer support. They handle warranty claims, production questions, and general inquiries. Response times can vary during peak Halloween season.

Lord Grimley's Manor

Our team consists of horror enthusiasts who collect masks ourselves. We offer:

  • Personalized recommendations based on your collection
  • Detailed product photos and measurements on request
  • Expert advice on mask care and display
  • Responsive support from people who genuinely love horror

Bottom line: For technical product questions, TOTS has manufacturer expertise. For buying guidance, collection advice, and personalized service, Lord Grimley's delivers a collector-focused experience.

Who Should Buy from Trick or Treat Studios

TOTS direct is the right choice if:

  • You want a specific current-production TOTS item
  • You're pre-ordering new releases on launch day
  • You only collect TOTS brand products
  • You need manufacturer warranty support

Ideal TOTS customer: Collectors focused specifically on licensed horror masks who want direct manufacturer relationship.

Who Should Buy from Lord Grimley's Manor

Lord Grimley's is built for collectors who:

  • Want curated selection across multiple premium brands
  • Seek rare, discontinued, or hard-to-find masks
  • Value personalized service from fellow horror enthusiasts
  • Want exclusive masks not available anywhere else
  • Appreciate clearance deals and bundle values
  • Need expert guidance building their collection

Ideal Lord Grimley's customer: Serious horror collectors who want a curated experience, access to rare items, and service from people who share their passion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Lord Grimley's Trick or Treat Studios products authentic?

Absolutely. We're an authorized TOTS retailer. Every Trick or Treat Studios product we sell is 100% authentic with full manufacturer warranty.

Why are some masks more expensive at Lord Grimley's?

Current-production items are priced at MSRP. Rare or discontinued masks may carry collector premiums based on scarcity and demand—these are items you simply can't get from TOTS anymore.

Does Lord Grimley's have items TOTS doesn't?

Yes. Our Lord Grimley Exclusive line features original designs created specifically for our store. We also stock products from other manufacturers like Ghoulish Productions, plus rare masks from our collector network.

Should I buy pre-orders from TOTS or Lord Grimley's?

For new releases, TOTS typically has the earliest access. We receive stock shortly after and often have availability when TOTS sells out. For popular items, pre-ordering from both increases your chances of securing one.
