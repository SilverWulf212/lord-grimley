Shipping

How much does shipping cost?

We offer the following shipping options for US orders:

  • Free Standard Shipping on orders over $75
  • Standard Shipping: $6.99 (5-7 business days)
  • Expedited Shipping: $12.99 (3-5 business days)
  • Priority Shipping: $19.99 (2-3 business days)

International shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on destination and weight.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! We ship to most countries worldwide. International shipping typically takes 7-21 business days depending on destination. Please note that international customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes.

How long does processing take?

Most orders are processed and shipped within 1-2 business days. During peak Halloween season (September-October), processing may take 2-4 business days due to high volume.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for unworn, unused items in original packaging. Items must be in resalable condition with all tags attached.

Non-returnable items: Makeup, prosthetics, contact lenses, and clearance items marked "Final Sale."

How do I start a return?

To start a return:

  1. Email us at support@lordgrimley.com with your order number
  2. Include the reason for return and items being returned
  3. We'll send you a return authorization and shipping label
  4. Ship the item back within 7 days of receiving the label

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days of receiving the return.

Can I exchange for a different item?

We recommend returning the original item for a refund and placing a new order for the desired item. This ensures you get your new item as quickly as possible without waiting for the exchange to process.

What are Lord Grimley Exclusives?

Lord Grimley Exclusives are limited edition masks and props created exclusively for our store. These items are not available anywhere else and are produced in limited quantities for serious collectors.

What size are the masks?

Most latex masks are designed to fit adult heads with a circumference of 22-24 inches. Masks stretch slightly and many include adjustable straps. Check individual product descriptions for specific sizing notes.

Mask Care

How should I store my latex mask?

For best results:

  • Store on a wig head or stuffed with newspaper to maintain shape
  • Keep in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight
  • Avoid storing in plastic bags (latex needs to breathe)
  • Apply a light dusting of talcum powder to prevent sticking

How do I clean my mask?

After wearing:

  1. Wipe the inside with a damp cloth to remove sweat
  2. Let air dry completely before storing
  3. For the exterior, use a damp cloth with mild soap if needed
  4. Never use alcohol, solvents, or harsh chemicals
  5. Condition with silicone spray annually to maintain flexibility

Can I repaint or modify my mask?

Yes, but proceed with caution. Use acrylic paints designed for latex or flexible surfaces. Test on an inconspicuous area first. Note that modifications may void any returns or warranties.
