Returns & Refunds Policy

Your satisfaction is our priority

Quick Summary

  • ✓ 30-day returns on most items
  • ✓ Items must be unworn and unused
  • ✓ Original packaging required
  • ✓ Refunds processed within 5-7 business days

Return Eligibility

We want you to be thrilled with your purchase from Lord Grimley's Manor. If you're not completely satisfied, we accept returns within 30 days of delivery under the following conditions:

  • Item is unworn, unused, and in original condition
  • All original tags and packaging are intact
  • Item is in resalable condition
  • Proof of purchase (order confirmation or receipt)

Non-Returnable Items

For hygiene and safety reasons, the following items cannot be returned:

  • Special effects makeup and prosthetics
  • Contact lenses and costume teeth
  • Theatrical blood and body paint
  • Items marked "Final Sale" or "Clearance - No Returns"
  • Gift cards
  • Custom or personalized items
  • Items that have been worn, used, or altered

How to Start a Return

  1. Contact Us

    Email support@lordgrimley.com with:

    • Your order number
    • Item(s) you wish to return
    • Reason for return

  2. Receive Return Authorization

    We'll send you a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number and return shipping label within 1-2 business days.

  3. Pack Your Return

    Securely pack the item in its original packaging. Include the RMA number inside the package.

  4. Ship It Back

    Drop off the package at any UPS location using the provided label. Keep your tracking receipt.

  5. Receive Your Refund

    Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll process your refund within 5-7 business days.

Refund Information

Refund Method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method:

  • Credit/Debit Card: 5-10 business days to appear on your statement
  • PayPal: 3-5 business days
  • Shop Pay: 3-5 business days

Refund Amount

  • Full product price refunded for items in original condition
  • Original shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error
  • Return shipping is free for defective items or our errors; otherwise, a $6.99 return shipping fee is deducted

Exchanges

We don't offer direct exchanges. For the fastest service:

  1. Return your original item for a refund
  2. Place a new order for the desired item

This ensures you get your new item as quickly as possible without waiting for the return to be processed.

Defective or Damaged Items

If you receive a defective or damaged item:

  1. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery
  2. Include photos of the damage and packaging
  3. We'll send a replacement or full refund at no cost to you

Defective items may be returned up to 60 days from delivery with photo documentation.

Wrong Item Received

If you received the wrong item, please contact us immediately. We'll:

  • Send a prepaid return label
  • Ship the correct item right away (if in stock)
  • Provide a full refund if the item is no longer available

Late or Missing Refunds

If you haven't received your refund after 10 business days:

  1. Check your bank/credit card statement again
  2. Contact your credit card company (refunds can take time to post)
  3. Contact your bank
  4. If you've done all this and still haven't received your refund, contact us

Questions?

If you have any questions about returns or refunds, please don't hesitate to contact our support team. We're here to help make things right!
