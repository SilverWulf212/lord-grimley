Shipping Policy

Getting your horrors to you

Free Shipping

We offer FREE Standard Shipping on all orders over $75 within the continental United States. No code required – discount applied automatically at checkout.

US Shipping Options

Shipping Method Delivery Time Cost
Standard Shipping 5-7 Business Days $6.99 (Free over $75)
Expedited Shipping 3-5 Business Days $12.99
Priority Shipping 2-3 Business Days $19.99
Overnight Shipping 1 Business Day $29.99

*Delivery times are estimates and do not include processing time. Alaska, Hawaii, and US territories may require additional time.

International Shipping

We ship to most countries worldwide! International shipping rates and delivery times vary by destination and are calculated at checkout based on package weight and dimensions.

Estimated International Delivery Times:

  • Canada: 7-14 business days
  • UK & Europe: 10-21 business days
  • Australia & New Zealand: 14-28 business days
  • Rest of World: 14-35 business days

Customs & Import Duties

International orders may be subject to customs duties, import taxes, and fees imposed by the destination country. These charges are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in our shipping costs. We recommend checking with your local customs office for more information.

Order Processing

  • Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST on business days typically ship the same day
  • Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST or on weekends ship the next business day
  • During peak Halloween season (September-October), processing may take 2-4 business days
  • Pre-order items ship when they become available (see product page for estimated dates)

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with tracking information. You can also track your order by:

  • Logging into your account and viewing order history
  • Using our Order Lookup tool with your order number and email
  • Contacting us at support@lordgrimley.com

Shipping Restrictions

Some items may have shipping restrictions due to size, weight, or content. These include:

  • Large props and animatronics (US shipping only)
  • Items containing lithium batteries
  • Aerosol products (spirit gum, blood spray, etc.)

Restricted items will be noted on the product page.

Lost or Damaged Packages

If your package is lost or arrives damaged:

  1. Contact us within 7 days of the expected delivery date
  2. For damaged items, please photograph the packaging and item
  3. We will file a claim with the carrier and work to resolve the issue
  4. Replacement or refund will be issued once the claim is processed

We recommend purchasing shipping insurance for high-value orders. This option is available at checkout.

Questions?

If you have any questions about shipping, please contact us or check our FAQ page.
